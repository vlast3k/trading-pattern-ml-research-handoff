## Advisor Confirmation Sensitivity

This models a human-supervised signal workflow: the strategy alerts, a human may miss some signals, and accepted signals enter after a configured bar delay. It is a research model for manual confirmation viability, not a way to disguise automation.

Delay is measured in the analyzed timeframe's bars, so `delay=1` means about 1 minute on 1m, 5 minutes on 5m, and 15 minutes on 15m. Signals expire after 2 delayed bars. Max chase skips entries that moved more than 0.50R in the signal direction before the manual entry.
